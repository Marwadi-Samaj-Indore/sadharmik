The tokens, measured

Contrast is computed by this page, not copied in. 4.5 : 1 is the floor for text people have to read; 3 : 1 for large text and icons.

Two rules, shown

One. Saffron is a plate, an accent and a mark. It never carries white words, and it is never itself a word on cream. Where saffron has to say something, it darkens.

Two. The samaj's logo carries the colour. It is already a full rainbow of petals, so everything around it stays quiet and kesar-led, and the two never compete. The logo appears where identity matters: here on the sign-in page, in the Home header, on the welcome sheet and under Me.
